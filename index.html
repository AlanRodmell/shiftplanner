Safe-Staffing & Auto-Scheduler Rules

• Day Core Coverage: Satisfied by staff marked as Core Staff working any DAY category shift (e.g., LD, E, L).
• Night Core Coverage: Satisfied by Core Staff working any NIGHT category shift (e.g., N).
• WTD Rest Protection: A staff member working a Night (N) shift will never be auto-assigned a Day shift the immediately following morning.
